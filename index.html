Scene 1 · The Business Challenge · Pune, India

One delay.
A business at risk.

A critical material delivery is late. Production is waiting. Someone needs to act.

Start Experience
New message · Supplier08:42 IST

Dear Procurement Team,
we regret to inform you that the materials scheduled for delivery yesterday have been delayed. We will share an updated delivery date shortly.

Your decision

1. What information would you check first?

Start with the facts of this order: what was promised, how much, and when. Everything else depends on it.

2. Who needs to be informed?

The people whose work depends on this material need to know early, so they can plan around it.

3. What should happen next?

Waiting wastes time and cancelling is premature. Good teams assess the impact and prepare options.

Scene 3 · What is an AI Agent?

Give it a goal. Not a script.

An AI agent is software that works towards a business goal. It understands the situation, decides what to do, takes action and learns from the result, with people kept in control.

The goal we gave our agent

“Keep production running despite supplier delays.”

Why oversight matters

Agents can be wrong, miss context or work from outdated data. Responsible use keeps people accountable for decisions that carry cost or risk.

ApprovalHumans sign off on high-impact actions.
TransparencyThe agent explains why it recommends something.
Data careOnly the data needed is used, and it is protected.
Audit trailEvery action is recorded and reviewable.
